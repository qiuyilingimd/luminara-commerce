# Luminara Storefront Direction

## Position

Luminara is a curated house for vintage-inspired statement jewellery. The first
edit combines decorative silhouettes with enamel, glass, resin, zircon and
metal-tone finishes at approximately US$35 to US$58. The primary customer is a
style-led shopper who mixes vintage or second-hand pieces with contemporary
clothes and uses jewellery to make a familiar outfit personal. The primary
action is to add a specific piece to the bag.

The core belief is **Taste is assembled**. A personal signature is built from
repeated choices, combinations and pieces worn often enough to become part of
the wearer.

Use "vintage-inspired" until the provenance of an item is confirmed. Production
location, handmade status, recycled content, edition sizes and firing processes
must not be published without evidence.

## Visual System

- Warm paper: `#F5F1E8`
- Ink: `#16110F`
- Oxblood: `#5B2025`
- Antique gold: `#A98243`
- Mineral turquoise: `#5F948B`
- Display: Playfair Display italic and roman
- Text and labels: Jost

Photography leads. The page alternates gallery-black fields with warm paper and
one oxblood close. Images remain uncropped where the jewellery silhouette
matters and move to macro crops where material is the subject.

The product system follows three evidence types: whole form, material surface
and worn scale. These are explained in the Assembly section and apply to the
Cabinet: every product plate uses the same 4:5 ratio and equal grid width so
silhouette, crop and finish can be compared directly.

## Page Rhythm

1. Autoplaying editorial film with direct shop action: find, hold, detail,
   pairing, assembly.
2. Plain product proposition built around “Taste is assembled.”
3. First product edit with four clear names and prices.
4. Three silent product studies: worn scale, surface and weight.
5. One black-and-white portrait.
6. The Assembly: whole form, surface and worn scale explained with product
   imagery.
7. Complete, filterable Cabinet.
8. Final purchase action and service information.

## Motion

The hero film loops without playback controls. It moves through material close
range, wearing scale and the assembled wardrobe, with a short material montage
between the slower wearable shots. Landscape and portrait are composed
separately and return to the opening ear close-up for the loop. A still-image
fallback keeps movement when browser or device policy refuses autoplay. GSAP is
limited to the title entrance, image reveals and scroll handoff. Motion supplies
spring-based product hover, filter arrivals, press feedback and drawer content
entrances. Lenis provides smooth scrolling. Reduced-motion users see still
posters and no decorative drift.

## Sound

The original ambient score uses a low harmonic bed, restrained glass tones and
slow harmonic changes rather than a song-like drum loop. It begins only after the
first browser interaction and loops at low volume. Product, menu and bag actions
use restrained procedural chimes. A small persistent sound switch controls the
experience.

## Avoid

- Spinner or generic loading copy.
- Pausing video controls in the page chrome.
- Oversized empty black screens with no product information.
- Reading-level difficulty caused by ornamental body type.
- Stock slogans, invented reviews or invented production claims.
