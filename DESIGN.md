# Luminara Storefront Direction

## Position

Luminara is a curated house for vintage-inspired statement jewellery. The first
edit combines six decorative silhouettes with enamel, glass, resin, zircon and
metal-tone finishes at approximately US$35 to US$58. The primary customer is a
style-led shopper who mixes vintage or second-hand pieces with contemporary
clothes and uses jewellery to make a familiar outfit personal. The primary
action is to add a specific piece to the bag.

The core belief is **Taste is assembled**. A personal signature is built from
repeated choices, combinations and pieces worn often enough to become part of
the wearer.

Use "vintage-inspired" until the provenance of an item is confirmed. Production
location, handmade status, recycled content, edition sizes and firing processes
must not be published without evidence.

## Visual System

- Warm ivory page: `#F2EBDD`
- Softer surface: `#FAF6EE`
- Highlight ivory: `#FFF9EF`
- Ink: `#16110F`
- Oxblood: `#68232F`
- Antique gold: `#9A713B`
- Muted warm brown: `#6B5B4E`
- Display: Cormorant Garamond roman and italic
- Text and labels: Jost

Photography leads the editorial chapters. The page alternates warm ivory fields,
a deep walnut-and-oxblood belief chapter, material-led product studies and an
oxblood closing field. Images remain uncropped where the jewellery silhouette
matters and move to macro crops where material is the subject.

The product system follows three evidence types: whole form, material surface
and worn scale. Cabinet plates use each product's original gallery photograph,
retaining its velvet, hand scale, surface and light rather than replacing it
with an isolated cutout. Plates use a deliberate mix of 4:5 and 16:10 crops so
the eye moves between earrings, bracelets and necklaces instead of reading a
repeated catalogue grid. Drawer and bag imagery keep the same original
photographs. The butterfly product has been removed from the visual system.

## Page Rhythm

1. A single still-life hero led by pink flower earrings with purple glass drops
   on black thorn branches against a blue sky, followed by a restrained
   “The bloom and the thorn.” editorial overlay.
2. A walnut-and-oxblood “Taste is assembled.” belief chapter: quiet note, large
   centred statement and a single glass-shelf earring study.
3. A single Two Doves still life with three inspectable material details.
4. A complete, filterable six-piece Cabinet using the original product
   photography and varied editorial scale.
5. A worn-scale image from the supplied blue-thorn campaign, used as context
   rather than repeated as a cover.
6. An oxblood closing statement and a dark warm footer with service information.

## Motion

The homepage opens on one static jewellery image. Landscape and portrait are
composed separately so the earrings remain complete at both sizes. The mobile
hero uses a 4:5 composition and the following proposition drops its long body
copy to protect the image-led rhythm. The belief chapter reveals note, statement
and image as one short editorial
sequence, with a restrained vertical camera drift in the photograph. Product
photography responds through a restrained hover scale while the jewellery stays
sharp and stable. Motion supplies
spring-based product hover, filter arrivals, press feedback and drawer content
entrances. Lenis provides smooth scrolling. Reduced-motion users see the full
page instantly with no decorative drift.

## Sound

The original ambient score uses a low harmonic bed, restrained glass tones and
slow harmonic changes rather than a song-like drum loop. It begins only after the
first browser interaction and loops at low volume. Product, menu and bag actions
use restrained procedural chimes. A small persistent sound switch controls the
experience.

## Avoid

- Spinner or generic loading copy.
- Pausing video controls in the page chrome.
- Oversized empty black screens with no product information.
- Reading-level difficulty caused by ornamental body type.
- Stock slogans, invented reviews or invented production claims.
