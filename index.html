The materialsMade by hand

Cut, cast, fired and set by hand.

Every piece begins as a wax model at the bench. Recycled brass is cast, enamel is fired at 780°C, and glass and zircon are set last. Slight differences in colour and surface are part of each numbered piece.

Notes from the bench

One degree too hot and the turquoise turns grey.

Enamel goes into the kiln three times. Between firings the piece is filed back by hand, so each cell keeps a thin dark edge — the line you see inside the snake, the wing, the lilac petals.

Chapter III · The atelier

Made by hand,
in one room.

Nothing here is assembled from a catalogue. Wax is carved, recycled brass is cast, enamel is fired until the colour sinks, and the final polish is done with a cloth over the knee.

  • CarveA wax model is cut at the bench, usually three times, before anything is cast.
  • FireEnamel goes into the kiln three times at 780°C. One degree more and the turquoise turns grey.
  • SetStones are set last, by hand, with the piece still warm from the previous polishing.

Made to be worn for years.

Seven numbered pieces from $35 to $58 USD. Orders ship tracked from Los Angeles, and duties are confirmed before payment.
